About this simulation

A built-in, repeatable four-way intersection model with vehicle arrivals, queue lengths, approach wait, signal phases, lane capacity, and a fixed-time baseline. It runs without SUMO. Routine scenarios include two simulated ambulances, while Emergency Response includes four from different approaches; the road is labeled on the map and receives the next safe green. Choose peak, incident, rain, or emergency conditions and change the seed for repeatable runs.

Model details

ML-assisted mode uses the Random Forest loaded from traffic_rf_model.pkl by the local Python backend. Its training target imitates a hand-built synthetic heuristic; speed and occupancy are estimated from queue length. Results do not represent live road conditions.
